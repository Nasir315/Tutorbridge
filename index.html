How it works

1

Pick a tutor

Filter by subject, grade, board, language, price and time slot. Every profile shows college, verified badge and ratings.

2

Book a trial class

The first class is a trial. If it is not a match, switch tutors. Time zones are matched automatically for kids abroad.

3

Join with one link

A Meet or Zoom link is created inside TutorBridge. No phone numbers are shared. Parents track progress on a dashboard.

Built for parents who worry

College ID verification

Tutors upload a college ID and pass a short demo lesson before going live.

Recorded sessions

Parents can review any class. Recordings are private and deleted on a schedule.

No contact sharing

All chat and calls stay on the platform. Phone numbers are never shown.

Parental consent

A parent creates and controls the child's account, in line with India's DPDP Act.
